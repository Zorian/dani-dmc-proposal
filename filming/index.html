The VSL3 to 4 minutes

The main video at the top of the website. Its one job: make her feel understood, then get her to take the free quiz.

Why it matters

This is the first thing every woman sees on the site. You have about 5 seconds to make her feel seen before she scrolls.

How it should feel

Warm, honest, big sis energy. You're not selling, you're telling her the truth she needs to hear.

Filming note: landscape, framed wide, so we can also crop a vertical version for Reels.

Pick your hook

Choose your favourite and film your top 2. We'll test which one keeps people watching longer.

  1. "If you've ever talked yourself out of the outfit, the meeting, the date… this is for you."
  2. "You're not shy. You're not lazy. You've just been shrinking for so long it feels normal."
  3. "What if the confident version of you isn't someone new… she's just who you were before life got loud?"
  4. "If you're tired of waiting to feel confident 'one day'… stay with me for 3 minutes."

Her pain

Say what she's thinking better than she can, and she trusts you have the answer.

  • Comparing yourself to everyone online

Your story

You're relatable because you've been her. Share only what you're comfortable with, in your own words. A line or two, about the choice, not the dark moment.

  • "I know because I was her."
  • Your low point, in your words
  • "I had two choices: give up on myself, or back myself like never before. I chose me."
  • "That was my rebirth."

Quiz reveal videos6 clips, 45 to 60 seconds each

The video she sees when she gets her Confidence Type. One for each type.

Why it matters

She's just answered personal questions, so curiosity is at its peak. This video has to make her feel "she gets me", then move her to the next step.

How it should feel

Like you're talking to just her. Warm and specific, never generic. The text is already on the page, so don't read it out. The video adds you.

Filming note: vertical. Change one small thing per video (a top, a seat, an angle) so each feels recorded just for her.

Quick wins 6 clips

+

Free value they save and come back to. Start each one with "Try this today."

  • People Pleaser: next time someone asks you for something, say "Let me check and get back to you."
  • Fixer: ask "Do you want my help, or do you want me to listen?"
  • Overthinker: make one small decision in under two minutes, and don't go back to it.
  • Hider: keep one photo of yourself you'd normally delete. Look at it once a day this week.
  • Imposter: write down three wins from this year. Read them before your next big moment.
  • Main Character: write down the one place you still shrink. That's your next level.

Video testimonials

For your past clients, not you. They film themselves on their phone, no videographer needed.

Why it matters

A real face and a real voice are hard to doubt. One 30 second clip from a past client beats any written review.

Before we use the written ones

Check Taylor, Jaleesa and Jada are still happy to be shown with their names and ages.

The message to send them

Hey love 💛 I'm launching something new and your words meant so much to me back then. Would you be up for filming a quick 30 to 60 second video on your phone about your experience? No script, just talk like you're telling a friend. I'll send you 5 questions to guide you. It would mean the world 🙏

The 5 questions

  1. Where were you before you joined?
  2. What made you say yes?
  3. What changed? (one specific moment)
  4. How do you feel now?
  5. What would you tell a woman who's thinking about it?

Tips for them: hold the phone vertically, face a window, no script, talk like you're telling a friend.
