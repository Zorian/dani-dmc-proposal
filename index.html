The funnel

Step one is free value and a community. Step two is the paid offer, which we will shape together once the community is live. Tap a step for detail.

The monthly rhythm

The free workshop becomes a fixed date women plan around. Same rhythm every month, so it gets easier each time.

Week 1

Announce

Theme revealed. Poster drops, stories go up, comment to join opens. Questions collected from DMs.

Week 2

Build up

Short videos on the theme, a podcast mention, reminder emails and a countdown in stories.

Week 3

Workshop night

You go live for about 75 minutes: your story, the topic, last month's questions, then live Q&A.

Week 4

Follow up

Replay sent, best moments clipped into content, community welcomes new members, wins shared.
